FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does JobMatch cost anything for students?

No. JobMatch is free for students and you keep your full wage. Employers pay a fee per successfully completed shift.

Do I need a CV?

No. You create a profile once with your availability, location and preferences, and apply to any job with a single swipe.

Do I get the job right after swiping?

A right swipe shows your interest, then the employer confirms the match. For short-term shifts this often happens the same day.

Where is JobMatch available?

We are starting with a pilot in Munich. More cities will follow once the model has proven itself there.

What kind of jobs are there?

Mainly short-term shifts and one-day jobs in hospitality, events and retail, wherever help is needed at short notice.

How are employers checked?

Employers are verified and rated by students after every shift, so you can see upfront who pays fairly and is well organised.
